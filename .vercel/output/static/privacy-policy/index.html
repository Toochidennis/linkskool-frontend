Privacy Policy

Last updated: January 23, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how Linkskool ("we", "our", "us") collects, uses, stores, shares, and protects personal data when you use the Linkskool mobile application, desktop application, and related services (collectively, the "Service").

We are committed to protecting your privacy and handling your personal data transparently, securely, and in compliance with applicable data protection laws, including the General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR) and the Nigeria Data Protection Act (NDPA).

1. Interpretation and Definitions

Interpretation

Words with capitalized initial letters have meanings defined below, whether used in singular or plural form.

Definitions

  • Account – A unique account created to access the Service.
  • Application – The Linkskool mobile and desktop software.
  • Company – Linkskool Online Ventures Limited, No. 86 Ogui Road, opposite Stadium, Enugu, Nigeria.
  • Device – Any device capable of accessing the Service.
  • Personal Data – Any information relating to an identified or identifiable individual.
  • Usage Data – Data collected automatically from the Service.
  • Service Provider – Any third party that processes data on our behalf.
  • You – The individual or legal entity using the Service.

2. Legal Basis for Processing (GDPR)

Under GDPR, we process personal data only where we have a lawful basis, including:

  • Your explicit consent
  • Performance of a contract
  • Compliance with legal obligations
  • Legitimate interests, where they do not override your rights

3. Data We Collect

3.1 Personal Data

  • Full name
  • Email address
  • Phone number
  • Account profile information (profile picture, bio, preferences)
  • Payment information (processed securely by third-party payment providers)

3.2 Usage Data

Usage Data may include IP address, device identifiers, operating system, browser type, application version, access times, pages viewed, and interaction logs with courses and content.

3.3 Device Permissions

With your permission, we may access your device camera or media library to support features such as profile images or educational content uploads.

You may revoke these permissions at any time via your device settings.

4. How We Use Your Data

  • To provide and maintain the Service
  • To authenticate users and manage accounts
  • To deliver educational content and features
  • To process enrollments and payments
  • To communicate service-related updates, course announcements, and promotional materials (with your consent)
  • To improve performance, security, and user experience through analytics
  • To respond to support requests and inquiries
  • To comply with legal and regulatory requirements
  • To detect and prevent fraud, abuse, or security incidents

5. Data Sharing and Disclosure

We do not sell your personal data.

We may share data only with:

  • Trusted Service Providers under strict confidentiality agreements (e.g., payment processors, cloud hosting providers, email service providers)
  • Affiliates operating under this same Privacy Policy
  • Legal authorities where required by law, court order, or government regulation
  • Business successors in the event of a merger, acquisition, or sale of assets
  • With your consent for specific purposes you have authorized

6. Data Retention

We retain personal data only for as long as necessary to fulfill the purposes outlined in this policy, unless a longer retention period is required or permitted by law.

Specifically:

  • Active accounts: Data is retained while your account remains active
  • Inactive accounts: Data may be anonymized or deleted after 3 years of inactivity
  • Course completion records: Retained for 7 years for certification purposes
  • Payment records: Retained for 7 years as required by tax and financial regulations
  • Marketing communications: Retained until you unsubscribe or withdraw consent

7. How We Delete Your Data

7.1 Account Deletion

You may request deletion of your account at any time by:

  • Accessing your account settings and selecting "Delete Account"
  • Contacting our support team at privacy@linkskool.com
  • Sending a written request to our company address

7.2 Deletion Process

Upon receiving a valid deletion request:

  • Within 30 days: We will permanently delete your personal data from our active systems
  • Within 90 days: We will remove your data from backup systems
  • Exceptions: Some data may be retained if required by law (e.g., transaction records for tax purposes) but will be anonymized where possible

7.3 What Gets Deleted

  • Personal profile information (name, email, phone, photo)
  • Account credentials and authentication data
  • Course progress and learning history (unless certification records are legally required)
  • Saved preferences and settings
  • Communication history and support tickets (subject to legal retention requirements)

7.4 What May Be Retained

Some information may be retained in anonymized form for:

  • Aggregate analytics and research (with all personal identifiers removed)
  • Legal compliance (e.g., financial records for 7 years as required by law)
  • Security and fraud prevention (e.g., IP addresses for banned accounts)

8. International Data Transfers

Your data may be processed and stored in countries outside your country of residence, including Nigeria and other jurisdictions where our service providers operate.

Where data is transferred internationally, we ensure appropriate safeguards are in place through:

  • Standard Contractual Clauses approved by the European Commission
  • Adequacy decisions for countries with equivalent data protection standards
  • Binding corporate rules for transfers within our corporate group

9. Your Rights Under GDPR & NDPA

You have the following rights regarding your personal data:

  • Right to Access: Request a copy of your personal data we hold
  • Right to Rectification: Correct inaccurate or incomplete data
  • Right to Erasure: Request deletion of your data (subject to legal obligations)
  • Right to Restrict Processing: Limit how we use your data in certain circumstances
  • Right to Object: Object to processing based on legitimate interests or for direct marketing
  • Right to Data Portability: Receive your data in a structured, machine-readable format
  • Right to Withdraw Consent: Withdraw consent for processing at any time (without affecting prior lawful processing)
  • Right to Lodge a Complaint: File a complaint with the Nigeria Data Protection Commission or your local data protection authority

To exercise any of these rights, contact us at privacy@linkskool.com

10. Security Measures

We implement industry-standard security measures to protect your data, including:

  • Encryption of data in transit (TLS/SSL) and at rest
  • Regular security audits and vulnerability assessments
  • Access controls and authentication mechanisms
  • Employee training on data protection and security best practices
  • Incident response procedures and breach notification protocols

Important: While we implement reasonable safeguards, no system is completely secure. We cannot guarantee absolute security of data transmitted over the internet.

11. Children's Privacy

Linkskool is not intended for children under 13 years of age without explicit parental or guardian consent.

If we become aware that we have collected personal data from a child under 13 without verified parental consent, we will take steps to delete that information promptly. Parents or guardians who believe we have collected information from their child may contact us at privacy@linkskool.com.

12. Third-Party Links and Services

Our Service may contain links to third-party websites, services, or content not operated by Linkskool.

We are not responsible for the privacy practices of third parties. We encourage you to review the privacy policies of any third-party services you access through our platform.

13. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time to reflect changes in our practices, legal requirements, or service features.

When we make material changes, we will:

  • Update the "Last updated" date at the top of this policy
  • Notify you via email or through a prominent notice in the Service
  • Request your renewed consent where required by law

Your continued use of the Service after changes become effective constitutes acceptance of the updated policy.
